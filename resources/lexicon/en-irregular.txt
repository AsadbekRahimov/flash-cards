# Irregular English word forms for the dictation of the practice (docs/15 §3.4, docs/16 §5.2).
# Used by App\Domain\Practice\Support\EnglishLexicon next to en-common.txt: a typed word on this list is another word, not a slip of
# the pen, so the dictation counts it as a mistake. Unlike the words of en-common.txt these are NEVER the base of a spelling rule:
# `met` is a word, but `meting` and `metting` are not `met` + `ing`.
# Contents: the past tense and the participle of the irregular verbs (all the forms but the lemma), the irregular plurals and the
# irregular degrees of comparison. A form that is also a lemma with regular forms of its own (found, bound, ground, wound, saw, rose,
# bore, lay, spoke, rent) stays in en-common.txt, where `founded`, `grounded`, `roses` come from.
# Format: one word to a line, lower case, sorted in byte order (LC_ALL=C sort), no repeats, no blank lines; a line with # is a comment.
# Written for this project, not copied from anywhere. Keep it sorted: tests/Unit/Practice/EnglishLexiconTest.php checks both files.
